## TL;DR

These two aren't direct competitors — they solve different problems.

- **3M 60923** is a *combination cartridge*: organic vapor + acid gas + P100 particulate filter in **one bayonet-mounted unit**.
- **3M 6001** is an *OV-only cartridge*: no acid-gas sorbent, no built-in particulate filter. If particulates are present, you add a 5N11 N95 pre-filter with a 501 retainer, or step up to a 60921 / 60923 combination.

If the hazard mix includes acid gases or particulates, the 60923 is the right answer. If the work is pure organic vapor in a particulate-free environment (rare in industry), the 6001 alone is sufficient and cheaper.

## What each cartridge is

### 3M 60923 — combination OV / Acid Gas / P100
- **NIOSH approval:** `[VERIFY against NIOSH CEL]`
- Protects against: organic vapors, acid gases (HCl, SO₂, Cl, HF), and 99.97% of oil-resistant particulates.
- Filter element: combined OV + AG sorbent **plus** built-in P100 mechanical filter.
- One-piece installation — no separate filter needed.
- Typical jobs: chemical processing, metal pickling, semiconductor, water treatment, pulp & paper, any acid-gas environment with co-incident particulates.

### 3M 6001 — OV-only cartridge
- **NIOSH approval:** `[VERIFY against NIOSH CEL — TC-23C-####]`
- Protects against: organic vapors only.
- **No acid-gas protection.** **No built-in particulate filter.**
- If particulates are present, you add a 3M 5N11 N95 pre-filter retained by a 3M 501 cover; the assembly fits the same bayonet.
- Typical jobs: spray painting in particulate-free clean booths, solvent transfer in well-monitored areas, lab solvent handling with no co-incident dust/mist.

## Side-by-side

| | 3M 60923 | 3M 6001 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organic vapor | ✅ | ✅ |
| Acid gas (HCl/SO₂/Cl/HF) | ✅ | ❌ |
| Particulate filter | P100 built in | None — add 5N11 N95 pre-filter |
| Parts in the stack | 1 (cartridge) | 1 cartridge, or 3 with pre-filter (cartridge + 5N11 + 501) |
| Mount | 3M bayonet | 3M bayonet |
| Cost per cartridge | Higher | Lower base, add pre-filter cost |
| Failure points | One sealing interface | Multiple — more chances for misassembly |

## Which to choose

**Pick the 60923 if** acid gas is present, expected, or not ruled out — **or** if the environment has co-incident particulates. The single-piece design has fewer failure points and is easier to train on.

**Pick the 6001 if** the work is documented pure-OV with confirmed-absent particulates and acid gases. Even then, audit whether your facility can guarantee particulate absence shift-to-shift. Many programs standardize on the combination cartridge purely to remove that operational risk.

**Operational note:** mixing the 6001 with a 5N11 pre-filter is a valid NIOSH-approved configuration, but it adds two parts to inspect on every donning. Combination cartridges (60921 / 60923 / 60926) simplify training and inspection.

## Compatibility

Both use the 3M bayonet mount and fit the 6000, 6500, 7500 half facepieces and 6800/6900 full facepieces.
