## TL;DR

Both are reusable half-mask respirators from 3M. The **6000 Series**
(6100/6200/6300) is the **industry workhorse**: thermoplastic elastomer
face seal, lightweight, lowest cost in the 3M half-mask line, NIOSH-
approved. The **6500 Series** (6501/6502/6503 — plus 6501QL/6502QL/6503QL)
is the **mid-tier upgrade**: silicone face seal, low-profile design for
better lateral visibility, and the **Quick Latch (QL) drop-down option** on
QL variants for short between-task removal without doffing the head harness.

Both use the **same 3M bayonet mount** — every cartridge or filter that
fits one fits the other (and the 7500). Same sizes (S/M/L). The decision is
comfort, lateral visibility, the drop-down workflow, and budget.

## When to pick the 6000 Series

- High-volume PPE program where unit cost dominates.
- Occasional or short-duration use — single shifts, intermittent tasks.
- Workers comfortable with thermoplastic elastomer face seals.
- Programs already standardized on the 6000 (avoid mixing seal materials
  across the program).

## When to pick the 6500 Series

- Workers who repeatedly need to drop the mask for short tasks (talking
  on a radio, taking a sip) and want a QL variant — the drop-down is
  faster than a full doff/re-don and keeps the head harness positioned.
- Tasks requiring better **lateral visibility** — the low-profile 6500
  design intrudes less into peripheral vision than the wider 6000.
- Workers with sensitivity to thermoplastic elastomer who tolerate
  silicone better.
- Programs that want to keep the 6000's price discipline while moving the
  most-demanding wearers to a more comfortable seal.

## When to step up to the 7500 Series instead

- All-day or multi-shift wear where face-seal comfort drives compliance.
- Hot or humid environments — the **7500's cool-flow exhalation valve**
  reduces heat buildup and fogging more than either the 6000 or 6500.
- Workers with a history of failing fit tests on both the 6000 and 6500.

## The QL (Quick Latch) variant — what it actually does

The **QL** suffix (e.g. 6502QL) means a quick-latch drop-down feature: a
button at the front of the mask that releases the facepiece to swing down
to chest level while the head harness stays in place. It's intended for
short between-task interruptions — taking a drink, brief conversation,
moving between sub-tasks — where a full doff/re-don would waste seal
integrity and time.

It is **not** a substitute for properly doffing the respirator at the end
of a use period, and it doesn't change the underlying fit-test or seal
requirements. The seal must be re-checked when the mask is re-latched.

## Cartridges and filters — same lineup for both

Because all three half-mask series (6000 / 6500 / 7500) use the 3M bayonet
mount, you can pair any of them with:

- **OV cartridges:** 6001 (OV), 6003 (OV + AG)
- **Multi-gas:** 6006
- **Combination + P100:** 60921 (OV/P100), 60923 (OV+AG/P100), 60926
  (Multi-Gas/P100), 60927 (Multi-Gas + Mercury / P100)
- **P100 filters:** 2091, 2097, 2297, 7093
- **N95 pre-filters:** 5N11 with 501 retainer

A facility standardizing on 6500 facepieces does not have to re-spec its
cartridge inventory.

## Fit-testing

Both series **must** be fit-tested per 29 CFR 1910.134 before tight-fitting
use. The 6500's silicone seal and low-profile design often pass on faces
where the 6000 doesn't — but face shape, not series brand, is the deciding
factor. **Fit test, don't assume.**

## What this guide is — and isn't

Recommendation grounded in NIOSH approval scope, OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134, and
current 3M product positioning. **Not** a substitute for an OSHA-compliant
respiratory-protection program, hazard assessment, or — most importantly —
**quantitative or qualitative fit testing**. No half-mask is "the right
one" until it passes a fit test on the worker's face.

We do not perform destructive testing, lab filtration-efficiency testing,
