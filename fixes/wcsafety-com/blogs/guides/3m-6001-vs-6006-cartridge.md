## TL;DR

The 6001 is an **organic-vapor-only** cartridge. The 6006 is a **multi-gas/vapor** cartridge — its NIOSH approval covers OV plus acid gases, ammonia/methylamine, formaldehyde, and hydrogen sulfide. Same bayonet, same physical size. Neither has a built-in P100; add a 5N11 pre-filter if particulates are present.

If you have a single contaminant (pure OV) the 6001 is sufficient and cheaper. For everything else — and for any mixed or uncertain hazard profile — the 6006 is the conservative answer.

## What each cartridge protects against

### 3M 6001 — Organic Vapor
- **NIOSH approval:** `[VERIFY against NIOSH CEL — TC-23C-####]`
- Protects against organic vapors only (solvents, fuels, paint vapors).
- Does **not** protect against acid gases, ammonia, methylamine, formaldehyde, hydrogen sulfide, or particulates.
- Typical jobs: solvent transfer in particulate-free clean areas, lab solvent work, light spray in well-ventilated booths.

### 3M 6006 — Multi-Gas / Vapor
- **NIOSH approval:** `[VERIFY against NIOSH CEL — TC-23C-####]`
- Protects against: OV class, acid gases (HCl, SO₂, Cl, HF), ammonia, methylamine, formaldehyde, hydrogen sulfide. `[VERIFY full gas list against current 3M TDS]`
- Does **not** include a particulate filter — pair with a 5N11 pre-filter if particulates co-occur.
- Typical jobs: chemical processing with mixed exposures, refrigeration system work (NH₃), wastewater (H₂S), embalming/laboratory formaldehyde exposure, semiconductor fabs.

## Which to choose

**Pick the 6001 if** the work is documented pure-OV in a particulate-free environment.

**Pick the 6006 if** any of the multi-gas contaminants is present, expected, or not ruled out. Ammonia exposure alone — common in refrigeration, agriculture, cold storage — is enough to require the 6006 over the 6001.

**Default to the 6006 in mixed-use facilities.** Standardizing on the broader cartridge simplifies your respiratory-protection program and removes the operational risk of someone grabbing the wrong cartridge mid-shift.

**If particulates are also present,** consider stepping up to the 60926 combination cartridge — it includes P100 + the same multi-gas coverage in one piece, reducing parts to inspect.

## Compatibility

Both use the 3M bayonet mount and fit the 6000, 6500, 7500 half facepieces and 6800/6900 full facepieces. They are mount-interchangeable.
