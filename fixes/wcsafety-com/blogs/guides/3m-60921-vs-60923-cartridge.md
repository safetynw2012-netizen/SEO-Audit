## TL;DR

The 3M 60921 protects against **organic vapors + particulates (P100)**. The 3M 60923 adds **acid gas** protection to the same OV + P100 coverage. If the work environment has any detectable acid gas — hydrogen chloride, sulfur dioxide, chlorine, hydrogen fluoride — the 60923 is the minimum. If the environment is pure organic vapor (solvents, paints, fuels) with particulates and **no** acid gases documented, the 60921 is sufficient and cheaper.

When in doubt and acid-gas presence isn't ruled out by air monitoring or the SDS, **default to the 60923**.

## Which to choose

**Pick the 60921 if** the air-monitoring data and product SDSs confirm only OV-class vapors plus particulates, and acid gases are demonstrably absent.

**Pick the 60923 if** acid gases are present at *any* detectable level, expected, or not ruled out by current monitoring. The OSHA respiratory-protection program rule treats unconfirmed exposures conservatively for a reason.

**Default to the 60923 when uncertain** — the OV protection is identical and you've added a meaningful protection class for a small price difference.
