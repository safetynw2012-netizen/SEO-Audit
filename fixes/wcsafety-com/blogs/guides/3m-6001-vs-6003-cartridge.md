## TL;DR

The 6001 is an **organic-vapor cartridge**. The 6003 adds **acid-gas protection** — HCl, SO₂, Cl, HF — to the same OV coverage. Same bayonet mount, same physical form factor, same retrofit path. If acid gases are present, expected, or not ruled out by air monitoring, the 6003 is the minimum. Neither cartridge has a built-in P100; add a 5N11 pre-filter with a 501 retainer if particulates are present.

## What each cartridge protects against

### 3M 6001 — Organic Vapor
- **NIOSH approval:** `[VERIFY against NIOSH CEL — TC-23C-####]`
- Protects against: organic vapors per the OSHA OV class (solvents, fuels, paint vapors, adhesive vapors).
- Does **not** protect against acid gases, ammonia, methylamine, formaldehyde, or particulates.
- Typical jobs: solvent transfer in particulate-free areas, lab solvent handling, light spray work in clean booths.

### 3M 6003 — Organic Vapor + Acid Gas
- **NIOSH approval:** `[VERIFY against NIOSH CEL — TC-23C-####]`
- Protects against: organic vapors **and** acid gases (HCl, SO₂, Cl, HF). `[VERIFY full acid-gas list]`
- Same physical size and mount as the 6001 — a drop-in upgrade when acid gases are documented.
- Does **not** protect against ammonia, methylamine, formaldehyde, or particulates.
- Typical jobs: chemical processing where OV and AG co-occur, water treatment, metal etching, refrigeration system service (within scope).

## Which to choose

**Pick the 6001 if** your SDS and air-monitoring data confirm pure-OV exposure and acid gases are documented absent.

**Pick the 6003 if** any acid gas is present, expected, or not ruled out. The OSHA respiratory-protection rule treats unconfirmed exposures conservatively.

**Step up to a combination cartridge** (60921 OV/P100, 60923 OV+AG/P100, or 60926 multi-gas/P100) if particulates are also present. The combination cartridges have the P100 built in and are easier to inspect/train than 6001/6003 + 5N11 + 501 stacks.

## Compatibility

Both use the 3M bayonet mount and fit the 6000, 6500, 7500 half facepieces and 6800/6900 full facepieces. They are mount-interchangeable.
