## TL;DR

The 60921 is an **organic-vapor + P100** combination cartridge. The 60926 is a **multi-gas + P100** cartridge — it covers organic vapor *plus* acid gases, ammonia/methylamine, formaldehyde, and hydrogen sulfide. The decision is binary: if any of those multi-gas contaminants is present, the 60921 is non-compliant and you need the 60926. If the work is genuinely pure-OV with particulates, the 60921 is sufficient.

## What each protects against

### 3M 60921 — OV / P100
- **NIOSH approval:** `[VERIFY against NIOSH CEL]`
- Protects against organic vapors (solvents, fuels, paint vapors, adhesives) plus P100 particulates.
- Does **not** protect against acid gases, ammonia, methylamine, formaldehyde, or hydrogen sulfide.
- Typical jobs: spray painting, refinish, woodworking with solvent-based coatings.

### 3M 60926 — Multi-Gas / P100
- **NIOSH approval:** `[VERIFY against NIOSH CEL]`
- Protects against the OV class plus acid gases (HCl, SO₂, Cl, HF), ammonia, methylamine, formaldehyde, and hydrogen sulfide — plus P100 particulates. `[VERIFY full coverage list against current 3M TDS]`
- The broadest combination cartridge in 3M's P100 line.
- Typical jobs: mixed-hazard chemical work, wastewater, semiconductor fabs, refrigeration system service (NH₃), formaldehyde-using processes.

## Which to choose

**Pick the 60921 if** your SDS and air-monitoring data confirm pure OV exposure with particulates and the multi-gas list above is documented absent.

**Pick the 60926 if** *any* of the multi-gas contaminants is present, expected, or not ruled out — including ammonia (common in refrigeration and agriculture), methylamine, formaldehyde (lab and embalming work), or H₂S (oil & gas, wastewater).

**Default to the 60926 in mixed-use facilities** where one cartridge type has to cover multiple job categories. The OV protection is identical; you're buying optionality.

## Compatibility

Both cartridges use the 3M bayonet mount and fit the same facepieces — 6000, 6500, 7500 half facepieces and 6800/6900 full facepieces. They are mount-interchangeable on the same mask.
