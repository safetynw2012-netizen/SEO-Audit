## The short version

WC Safety is reader-supported. When you buy through links on this site we may earn a commission from the retailer, **at no extra cost to you**. We only recommend products we believe meet the safety standards described in our [editorial methodology](/pages/editorial-methodology). We never accept payment in exchange for a higher ranking.

## What "affiliate marketing" means here

We do not sell, ship, or warehouse products. When you click a "Check price", "Buy on Amazon", or similar link on WC Safety, you are sent to a third-party retailer's website where the purchase, payment, shipping, and any returns are handled by that retailer. If the retailer's affiliate program credits the purchase to WC Safety, we receive a commission — typically a small percentage of the sale price.

## How affiliate income affects what we recommend

It doesn't change our rankings — and we've designed our process so it can't:

1. **Rankings come first, links come after.** Editors choose and rank products against the criteria in our [editorial methodology](/pages/editorial-methodology) before the affiliate-link layer is added.
2. **Commission rates do not influence ranking.** A product earning us a 1% commission can rank ahead of one earning us 10%, and frequently does. We will not promote a product that fails our safety criteria, regardless of payout.
3. **No paid placement.** We do not accept payment in exchange for inclusion, ranking, or a positive review.
4. **Review samples are disclosed.** If a manufacturer sent us a free sample, the article will say so. Samples are returned, donated, or kept for follow-up testing — never sold for personal gain.
