## What we do — and what we don't

- **We are a review and recommendation site.** We do not manufacture safety equipment.
- **We are an affiliate marketer.** When you click a "Check price" or product link and buy from a partner retailer (Amazon, Grainger, Home Depot, and others), we may earn a commission. See our [affiliate disclosure](/pages/affiliate-disclosure).

## How we choose products

Our recommendations are governed by a written [editorial methodology](/pages/editorial-methodology). In short, every product in a "Best…" guide must:

1. Meet the relevant US safety standard (NIOSH 42 CFR 84 for respirators, ANSI/ISEA Z89.1 for hard hats, UL 217 for smoke alarms, UL 2034 for CO alarms, etc.).
2. Be available from at least one US retailer in the past 30 days.
3. Have been used or hands-on inspected by a member of our team, **or** be ranked based on documented criteria from manufacturer specifications, NIOSH/OSHA databases, and aggregated verified-purchase reviews — with the basis clearly labelled in the article.

If we have not personally tested a product, we say so.
