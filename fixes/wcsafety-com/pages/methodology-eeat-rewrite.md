# Methodology rewrite — for E-E-A-T "Experience" signal

Drop-in replacement for the "How we research products" / methodology
passage on the live About page. Goal: **demonstrate** experience instead
of disclaiming it — without fabricating any testing.

> **The core move:** WC Safety stocks and ships this equipment, so the
> units are physically in hand. That is legitimate first-hand *Experience*
> (the first "E" in E-E-A-T) and Google's product-reviews system rewards it
> — and it requires zero destructive or fit-factor testing. The old copy
> led with everything you DON'T do, which reads to Google as "no
> experience." This leads with what you genuinely DO.

> **Honesty gate — do not publish a claim that isn't true.** Every line

## How we research products

Because WC Safety stocks and ships the equipment we cover, most reviews
begin with the actual unit in our hands — not a spec sheet on a screen.
Every review and buyer's guide is built the same way:

1. **Hands-on inspection of the physical unit.** `[VERIFY: only for items
   you actually stock/handle]` We examine the products we carry directly —
   build quality and materials, the face seal and strap hardware,
   cartridge-seat and valve fit, molded certification markings, included
   components, and packaging. Our reviews use **our own photographs of the
   actual product**, not manufacturer stock images. `[VERIFY: only if the
   photos are genuinely yours]`

2. **Certification verified on the unit, against the source database.** We
   read the approval markings stamped on the product itself and
   cross-reference them — every respirator and cartridge against its NIOSH
   TC-approval number on the NIOSH Certified Equipment List; every CO and
   smoke alarm against its UL listing (UL 2034 / UL 217 / UL 2075). If the
   approval can't be verified, we don't sell it and we don't recommend it.

3. **Specifications cross-referenced against primary sources.** We check
   each product's documented performance against the manufacturer's
   technical data sheet and the governing standard — OSHA 29 CFR 1910
   exposure limits and program rules, ANSI/ASSE ratings (Z87.1 eye, Z89.1
   head, Z88.2 respiratory), and EPA / CPSC / IARC hazard data where
   chemical exposure is involved.

4. **Matched to the real use case.** We state which job site, hazard class,
   and user each product is actually right for — including when a
   competitor's product, a cheaper option, or something we don't stock is
   the better answer.

**The scope of our testing — stated plainly.** Our edge is rigorous,
primary-source verification by people who handle this gear every working
day. We are equally transparent about where that stops: we do **not**
perform destructive testing, laboratory filtration-efficiency testing, or
quantitative fit-factor measurement, and no WC Safety review is a substitute
for a workplace respiratory-protection program or a Certified Industrial
Hygienist's assessment. When a conclusion depends on data only lab or field
testing can produce, we cite NIOSH's or the manufacturer's published figures
and say so — we never imply we measured it ourselves.

**On every review** we publish a short "How this review was researched"
note: the unit we inspected, confirmation the photos are our own, the
approval numbers we verified and where, the primary sources cited, the
**named reviewer**, and the last-reviewed date. `[VERIFY: add these to each
review template]`

## Why this scores better

| Old framing | Rewrite | E-E-A-T effect |
| ----------- | ------- | -------------- |
| Opens with "not destructive testing… not fit-factor… we don't fabricate" | Opens with hands-on inspection + **own photos** | Signals **Experience** instead of its absence |
| Disclaimer is the whole message | Disclaimer is a confident scope statement at the end | Keeps honesty; stops it from defining the page |
| Generic "spec audit" | Names the on-unit checks (read the TC label, cross-ref the CEL) | Demonstrable **Expertise**, not a claim |
| No per-article attribution | "named reviewer" + per-review research note | Ties methodology to **person-level authority** |

**Biggest single lever inside this rewrite:** *our own photographs of the
actual product*. Original images of the unit are one of the strongest
signals in Google's product-reviews system, they're impossible to fake at
scale, and as a stocking retailer you can produce them truthfully today.

**Projected impact:** publishing this (with the `[VERIFY]` lines made true)
plus naming a real reviewer would move Content & E-E-A-T from **78 (C+)
toward ~85 (B)** once crawled. Score unchanged until then — it reflects the
live, indexed page.
