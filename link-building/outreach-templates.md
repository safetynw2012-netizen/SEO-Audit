# Outreach templates

Rules that apply to every template below:

- Fill every `{{variable}}`. An unfilled variable that ships reads as a
  mail merge and gets deleted.
- The specific detail in the first two sentences is what earns the read.
  If you cannot name something real about their page, you have not
  qualified the prospect — go back to `prospecting.md` §4.
- Subject lines: lowercase, specific, no "quick question", no "collab".
- Never attach anything on first contact.
- Disclose the affiliate relationship to .gov / .edu / non-profit
  prospects. It is in every template below for a reason: keep it there.
- Send from a real person at the domain, with a signature naming them.
- One follow-up at ~7 days, one at ~18 days, then stop.

## A. Resource-page pitch (tactic T2)

**Subject:** `{{asset_topic}} resource for your {{page_name}} page`

```
Hi {{name}},

I was going through your {{page_name}} page at {{page_url}} — the
{{specific_thing_on_their_page}} is a genuinely useful pointer, and it's
the reason I'm writing rather than a generic list.

I put together {{asset_title}} ({{asset_url}}): {{one_sentence_on_what_it_is
_and_why_it_is_different_from_what_they_already_link_to}}. It's sourced
from {{standards_bodies_cited}} and there are no product links on the page.

If it's useful to the people you're pointing at that page, I'd be glad to
see it added. If not, no reply needed.

One thing you should know either way: WC Safety earns affiliate
commissions on some other pages of the site. The {{asset_title}} page
itself carries no affiliate links, and our methodology is at
{{methodology_url}} if you want to check how we work.

{{sender_name}}
{{sender_title}}, WC Safety
{{sender_email}}
```

**Follow-up 1 (day 7):**

```
Hi {{name}} — following up once on the {{asset_title}} resource below,
in case it got buried. Happy to hear a no; I won't chase further after
this. {{sender_name}}
```

## B. Broken-link replacement (tactic T3)

**Subject:** `broken link on {{page_name}}`

```
Hi {{name}},

Small heads-up: on {{page_url}}, the link to "{{dead_link_anchor}}"
({{dead_url}}) is returning {{404_or_redirect_detail}}. I checked it
{{date_checked}} — looks like {{likely_cause, e.g. "CDC reorganised
that section in 2023"}}.

The closest current source I know of is {{authoritative_replacement_url}}.
We also maintain {{asset_title}} ({{asset_url}}), which covers
{{what_it_covers}} — use whichever fits, or neither.

For transparency: WC Safety is an affiliate site, though that asset page
carries no affiliate links.

{{sender_name}}
{{sender_title}}, WC Safety
```

Lead with their broken link and offer the neutral authoritative source
first. That is what makes this a favour rather than a pitch, and it is
why it converts — do not reorder it.

## C. Unlinked-mention reclamation (tactic T4)

**Subject:** `thanks for the WC Safety mention`

```
Hi {{name}},

specifically the bit about {{what_they_said}}. Appreciated.

If it's an easy edit, would you mind linking that mention to
{{most_relevant_url}}? It'd help readers who want the full

{{sender_name}}
{{sender_title}}, WC Safety
```

## D. Expert-sourcing response (tactic T5)

For Qwoted / Featured / SourceBottle / direct reporter requests. Reply
inside the deadline or do not reply — journalists work to the hour.

```
{{Direct answer to the question asked, 2–4 sentences, no preamble.}}

{{One concrete, checkable specific: a standard number, an APF value, a
placement clearance, a failure mode you have actually seen.}}

Attribution: {{full_name}}, {{credential_and_issuing_body}}, {{title}} at
WC Safety ({{wcsafety_url}}). {{one_line_on_relevant_hands_on_experience}}.

Disclosure: WC Safety is an affiliate publisher and earns commissions on
product links. I have no relationship with any manufacturer named above.

Reachable at {{phone}} / {{email}} through {{deadline}}.
```

**Do not send this template without a real credentialed person behind
it.** Inventing an expert for a respirator or CO-detector story is
fraud, not marketing, and it is the specific failure mode this niche
punishes hardest.

## E. Contributed article pitch (tactic T7)

**Subject:** `pitch: {{article_angle}}`

```
Hi {{editor_name}},

{{One sentence showing you read the publication — name a recent piece
and what it argued.}}

Pitch: {{working_title}}. {{Two or three sentences: the argument, why it
matters to their readership now, and what makes it different from
{{the piece they already ran on this}}.}}

I can bring {{what you actually have: test observations, a standards
walkthrough, survey data}}. {{word_count}} words, drafted by
{{delivery_date}}.

Byline would be {{full_name}}, {{credential}}. I write at WC Safety
({{wcsafety_url}}), an affiliate publisher in the PPE space — happy to
keep the piece free of any product we monetise, and I'd expect you'd
want that anyway.

{{sender_name}}
```

No payment offered, none accepted, in either direction. If an outlet
asks for a placement fee it is not an outlet — see `playbook.md` §2.
